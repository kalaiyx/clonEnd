🏆 clonEnd Presentation & Pitch Guide

Comprehensive technical breakdown matching your project presentation deck and defense jury questions.

1. Problem Statement

  • The Attack: Fraudsters extract high-resolution thumbprints from public land registration deed PDFs, cast silicone rubber molds, and withdraw cash at rural Micro-ATMs.
  • Zero OTP: AePS is designed without OTP so rural citizens can access banking, creating an open vector for cloned prints.
  • Vulnerable Demographic: Over 70% of victims are elderly pensioners and farmers who do not own smartphones or know how to lock Aadhaar on mAadhaar.

2. The 3-Lock Architecture

  • Lock 1: Alive Lock: Vision AI checks blood perfusion (Cr/Cb skin red-shift), micro-texture Laplacian variance, and specular glare.
  • Lock 2: Place Lock: Haversine spherical math flags impossible travel velocity (e.g. Trichy deed to Coimbatore in 10 minutes = 1,155 km/h).
  • Lock 3: Owner Lock: Automated Tamil voice IVR call to the customer's ₹1,000 basic 2G phone ("Press 1 to approve").
  • Convergence Rule: All 3 must pass for cash to dispense. Any failure triggers an immediate block and SMS warning.

3. Innovation & Uniqueness

  • No Smartphone or Internet Needed: Works seamlessly on 2G GSM cellular networks with any ₹1,000 basic button phone.
  • Zero Hardware Replacement: Runs on existing Micro-ATM laptops and terminals without requiring banks to buy expensive multi-spectral optical hardware.
  • Zero Marginal Cost: Uses open-source computer vision, trigonometric spherical math, and synthesized voice audio.

4. National Impact & Scale

  • Financial Inclusion: In FY2022, AePS processed 1.11 Billion withdrawals worth nearly ₹3 Trillion (RBI Report).
  • Zero Leakage: Prevents devastating cybercrime attacks while keeping Aadhaar biometric transactions accessible for every Indian citizen.

💡 Top Viva & Evaluator Questions

Q: Why doesn't UIDAI's existing mAadhaar biometric lock solve this?
A: mAadhaar requires a smartphone and digital literacy. Over 70% of rural AePS users only possess basic keypad phones.

Q: Why do optical scanners in shops accept silicone fingers?
A: Existing shop scanners are simple 2D optical sensors that only check ridge contrast, not biological blood perfusion or organic skin chrominance.

Q: How does Lock 3 work if the user has no internet connection?
A: Standard Telecom IVR operates over voice channels on 2G GSM cellular networks. Even a basic Nokia 105 or JioBharat phone receives calls and transmits DTMF tones.
