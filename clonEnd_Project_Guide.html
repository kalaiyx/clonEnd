1. The Problem: How Thumbprint Cloning Works

🇬🇧 English Explanation

The Scam: Public land registry deeds (Patta/Chitta) published online contain high-resolution inked thumb impressions (kai-naattu). Fraudsters download these PDFs, fabricate physical 3D silicone/rubber stamps, and withdraw cash at rural Bank Mitra Micro-ATMs.

The Security Loophole:

  • Zero OTP: AePS cash withdrawal does not send SMS or OTP.
  • No Smartphone: Over 70% of rural elderly beneficiaries only own 2G keypad phones and cannot use mAadhaar biometric lock.
  • Dumb Scanners: Basic shop scanners only check 2D optical contrast, unable to distinguish living warm tissue from dead silicone rubber.
🇮🇳 Thanglish Explanation

Enna Problem? Namma Government land pathiram (registration deed/patta) PDF internet-la irunthu download panni, athula irukkura vivasaayigaloda kai-naattu-ai silicone rubber / wax-la dummy duplicate viral (cloned thumb) ready panraanga.

Yen System Fail Aaguthu?

  • No OTP: Micro-ATM / Petti kadai-la thumb vecha udane ₹10,000 varaikkum kaasu thirudiraanga. OTP ethuvum varaathu!
  • Smart Phone Illa: Gramathu periyavangalukku Android phone illa, mAadhaar app-la lock panna theriyaathu.
  • Dummy Scanner: Kadaigal-la irukkura scanner machine-ku dummy silicone-kum unmaiyaana skin-kum vithiyaasam theriyaathu.

3. Detailed Breakdown of the 3 Security Locks

👁️ Lock 1: Alive Lock (Computer Vision & Bio-Liveness AI) core/alive_lock.py

How it works: Captures the finger at the kiosk using the webcam in <1 second. Examines multi-spectral human skin chrominance (YCrCb & HSV), capillary blood perfusion, and papillary micro-texture using Laplacian variance.

🇬🇧 English Details:
  • Living Skin: Human blood has high hemoglobin red absorption ($133 \le \text{Cr} \le 173$) and fine friction ridges ($80 \le \text{Laplacian} \le 2200$). Verified score: 68% – 95% (GREEN).
  • Silicone / Rubber: Flat texture, lacks organic blood diffusion, reflects localized specular glare points. Score: 51% (BLOCKED).
  • Paper Deed Printout: Flat white toner background, monochrome ink, no blood dispersion. Score: 0% (BLOCKED).
🇮🇳 Thanglish Details:
  • Camera Check: Kiosk camera viral-ai paathu, ithu ratham oodura unmaiyaana skin-aa illa dummy rubber-aa-nu 1 second-la kandupidikkum.
  • Silicone Kandupidikkum: Dummy rubber finger mela light patta plastic cover maathiri glitter aagum (glare). Blood flow irukkaathu.
  • Paper Printout: Land deed Xerox printout-la vella paper & black ink mattum irukkum. System udane 0% score pottu block pannidum.
📍 Lock 2: Place Lock (Geo-Location & Impossible Travel Check) core/place_lock.py

How it works: Uses the spherical Haversine Formula to measure great-circle distance between the registered deed/home city and current Micro-ATM kiosk, calculating implied travel speed: $\text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}}$.

🇬🇧 English Details:
  • Local Transaction: Registered in Trichy, withdraws in Trichy $\rightarrow$ Distance $0\text{ km}$, Speed $0\text{ km/h}$ $\rightarrow$ PASS (GREEN).
  • Impossible Travel: Registered in Trichy, attempted in Coimbatore ($220\text{ km}$) within $10\text{ mins}$ $\rightarrow$ Implied Speed = $\mathbf{1,155\text{ km/h}}$! Flags Impossible Travel Anomaly (BLOCKED).
🇮🇳 Thanglish Details:
  • Math Logic: Oru vivasaayi-oda land pathiram register aana ooru Trichy. Scammer antha thumb-ai eduthu 10 nimishathula Coimbatore-la cash edukka try panraan.
  • Speed Anomaly: Trichy to Coimbatore 220 km. 10 nimishathula poga speed 1,155 km/h aagum! Ground travel limit 120 km/h dhaan. System spot-la fraud nu RED flag panni block pannidum!
📞 Lock 3: Owner Lock (Tamil Voice Call on 2G Keypad Phone) core/owner_lock.py

How it works: Bridges the digital divide. Triggers an automated GSM IVR phone call to the owner’s basic ₹1,000 keypad phone, speaking in clear, friendly Tamil (via gTTS).

"வணக்கம் Ramasamy! உங்கள் ஆதார் எண் XXXX-XXXX-1011 பயன்படுத்தி, Coimbatore வங்கி மித்ரா மையத்தில் ரூபாய் 3,000 பணம் எடுக்க முயற்சி செய்யப்படுகிறது. நீங்கள் தான் பணம் எடுக்கிறீர்கள் என்றால் 1-ஐ அழுத்தவும். நீங்கள் இல்லை என்றால் 9-ஐ அழுத்தவும்."
🇬🇧 English Details:
  • Press 1: Owner authorized transaction $\rightarrow$ PASS (Approved).
  • Press 9: Owner alerted to scam $\rightarrow$ BLOCKED (Scam Aborted).
  • Timeout / No Answer: Phone unreachable $\rightarrow$ HELD FOR SAFETY.
🇮🇳 Thanglish Details:
  • No Smartphone Needed: Normal keypad phone-ku Tamil-la call poi kekkum.
  • 1 Amukkina: Unmaiyaana aal dhaan kaasu edukraar nu cash release aagum.
  • 9 Amukkina: Thappana aal edukraan nu therinju spot-la block aagidum!
⚖️ Master Decision Engine & Audit Logger core/decision_engine.py

Convergence Rule: All 3 locks must pass. If any lock fails, transaction is blocked, logged in data/fraud_logs.csv, and an automated Tamil emergency SMS is prepared for the victim.
